# Prototype.NEXT

**AI Transformation & Production Engineering**

Prototype.NEXT helps companies identify high-value AI opportunities, build the systems, and put them into production.

## Positioning

**From AI opportunity to production.**

We combine business transformation, AI engineering, and production software engineering to transform real workflows rather than sell isolated AI features.

## What we do

- AI Transformation
- AI Engineering
- AI Workflow Automation
- AI Agents and Copilots
- RAG and Knowledge Systems
- AI Product Engineering
- Production Software Engineering
- Evaluation, Observability, and Guardrails
- Continuous AI Systems Improvement

## Primary offer

### AI Transformation Sprint

A focused engagement designed to identify and validate the highest-value AI opportunity inside a business.

Typical outputs:
- workflow discovery;
- opportunity prioritization;
- solution architecture;
- prototype or implementation;
- ROI model;
- production roadmap.

Target commercial range: **$15K–$30K**.
