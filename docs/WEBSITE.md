# Website

## Primary objective

Generate qualified conversations for:
1. AI Transformation Sprint;
2. production implementation;
3. strategic AI engineering partnerships.

## Secondary objectives

- establish credibility;
- demonstrate engineering depth;
- communicate category and positioning;
- support outbound sales;
- provide case-study proof;
- create a foundation for content and SEO.

## Homepage information architecture

### 1. Hero

Primary direction:

**Build the AI-native version of your business.**

Supporting copy:

Prototype.NEXT helps companies identify high-value AI opportunities, engineer the systems, and put them into production.

Primary CTA:
**Start an AI Transformation**

Secondary CTA:
**See Our Work**

Alternative hero:
**From AI opportunity to production.**

## 2. Proof

Show:
- production engineering experience;
- platforms;
- capabilities;
- selected customers/projects where legally permitted;
- measurable results where verified.

## 3. Problem

Explain that companies often have:
- AI experiments but no production path;
- repetitive workflows;
- fragmented knowledge;
- overloaded teams;
- uncertain ROI;
- security/reliability concerns.

## 4. AI Transformation Sprint

Explain:
Discover → Prioritize → Architect → Prototype → Production Roadmap.

Primary CTA.

## 5. Capabilities

Keep to a small number:
- AI Transformation;
- AI Engineering;
- AI Product Engineering;
- Production Engineering.

## 6. Case studies

2–3 strong case studies.

Do not fill space with weak project cards.

## 7. How we work

Suggested sequence:

**Strategy → Prototype → Production → Scale**

Explain:
- measurable objectives;
- architecture;
- delivery;
- evaluation;
- continuous improvement.

## 8. Technology / model stance

Show model-agnostic credibility without becoming a logo wall.

Possible providers:
- OpenAI;
- Anthropic;
- Google;
- open-source;
- modern web/data/cloud stack.

## 9. Founder / engineering credibility

The site should make clear that Prototype.NEXT is led by experienced production engineers, not AI-marketing generalists.

## 10. Final CTA

**Find the highest-value AI opportunity in your business.**

CTA:
**Start an AI Transformation**

## Remove from the old positioning

Remove or heavily rewrite:
- generic ChatGPT-integration messaging;
- broad custom-software copy;
- generic AI benefit lists;
- coupon/subscription language;
- template-style agency claims;
- vague innovation copy.

## SEO direction

Initial topic clusters:
- AI transformation consulting;
- production AI engineering;
- AI workflow automation;
- AI agents for business;
- enterprise RAG;
- AI product engineering;
- LLM evaluation;
- AI implementation strategy.

SEO content must be useful to executive and technical buyers, not keyword filler.

## Conversion

Every major page should offer one clear next action:
- book a conversation;
- request a sprint;
- review a relevant case study.

Avoid competing CTAs.
