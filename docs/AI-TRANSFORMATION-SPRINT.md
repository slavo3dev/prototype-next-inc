# AI Transformation Sprint

## Purpose

The AI Transformation Sprint is Prototype.NEXT's primary entry offer.

It converts vague interest in AI into a prioritized, technically credible, economically justified implementation path.

## Core promise

**Find and validate the highest-value AI opportunity inside your business.**

A more aggressive version may be used once delivery is standardized:

**30 days. One measurable business workflow transformed.**

## Target client

Best suited to companies that:
- want to use AI but lack a clear roadmap;
- have meaningful operational workflows;
- have fragmented knowledge or manual processes;
- have already experimented with AI without production success;
- need an engineering partner, not just strategy slides.

## Typical duration

Approximately 3–4 weeks.

Duration may vary depending on company complexity and implementation scope.

## Phase 1 — Discover

Goals:
- understand the business;
- map key workflows;
- interview stakeholders;
- identify bottlenecks;
- identify repeated knowledge work;
- understand systems and data;
- identify constraints.

Outputs:
- workflow map;
- problem inventory;
- baseline metrics;
- candidate AI opportunities.

## Phase 2 — Prioritize

Each opportunity should be evaluated across:
- business value;
- frequency;
- labor/time cost;
- data availability;
- implementation complexity;
- risk;
- model suitability;
- time-to-value;
- strategic differentiation.

Outputs:
- opportunity matrix;
- recommended priority workflow;
- ROI hypothesis.

## Phase 3 — Architect

Define:
- system boundaries;
- data flow;
- models;
- tools;
- retrieval;
- APIs;
- integrations;
- security;
- human approval;
- evaluation;
- observability.

Outputs:
- architecture;
- implementation specification;
- risk register;
- evaluation plan.

## Phase 4 — Prototype / Implement

Depending on scope:
- build a functional prototype;
- integrate representative data;
- validate model quality;
- test workflow behavior;
- measure latency and cost;
- validate user experience.

The prototype must answer a business or technical uncertainty.

It is not a demo for its own sake.

## Phase 5 — Production Roadmap

Deliver:
- recommended production architecture;
- roadmap;
- milestones;
- implementation estimate;
- ownership model;
- operational risks;
- expected business impact;
- next engagement recommendation.

## Success metrics

The sprint should attempt to establish metrics such as:
- hours saved;
- response time reduced;
- throughput increased;
- support deflection;
- error reduction;
- revenue impact;
- employee capacity created;
- cost per workflow;
- model quality.

## Deliverables

A standard sprint should produce:
- executive summary;
- workflow analysis;
- AI opportunity map;
- prioritized use-case matrix;
- architecture diagram;
- prototype or implementation;
- evaluation results;
- ROI model;
- production roadmap;
- risk/governance notes.

## Commercial model

Target price:
**$15K–$30K**

The sprint should naturally lead to:
- implementation;
- production launch;
- ongoing AI Systems Partnership.
