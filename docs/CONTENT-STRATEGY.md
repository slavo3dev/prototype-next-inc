# Content Strategy

## Objective

Content should create trust with:
- founders;
- CEOs;
- COOs;
- CTOs;
- VP Engineering;
- product leaders.

The goal is not mass AI-news traffic.

The goal is to demonstrate how Prototype.NEXT thinks about production AI and business transformation.

## Content pillars

### 1. AI Transformation

Topics:
- how to identify high-value AI workflows;
- ROI frameworks;
- what should not be automated;
- AI operating models;
- choosing the first production use case.

### 2. Production AI Engineering

Topics:
- prototype vs production;
- evaluation;
- observability;
- latency;
- cost;
- security;
- human-in-the-loop;
- failure modes.

### 3. Agents and Workflow Systems

Topics:
- when agents are useful;
- when deterministic workflows are better;
- tool use;
- orchestration;
- approvals;
- agent reliability.

### 4. Knowledge and RAG

Topics:
- internal knowledge systems;
- retrieval quality;
- permissions;
- citations;
- document pipelines;
- evaluation.

### 5. AI Product Engineering

Topics:
- AI-native UX;
- product architecture;
- model selection;
- streaming responses;
- cost/quality tradeoffs;
- model abstraction.

### 6. Engineering Leadership

Topics:
- architecture;
- team productivity;
- AI-assisted engineering;
- agent instructions;
- documentation-as-context;
- codebase modernization.

## Formats

Priority:
- case studies;
- technical articles;
- executive guides;
- architecture breakdowns;
- short LinkedIn/X posts derived from deeper material;
- demonstrations.

## Publishing principle

One deep artifact should create multiple smaller pieces.

Example:

1. Publish a case study.
2. Extract an architecture post.
3. Extract a business-ROI post.
4. Extract a technical failure/lesson.
5. Extract a short social post.
6. Use the case study in outbound.

## Content quality bar

Every piece should provide at least one of:
- a useful framework;
- an architectural insight;
- a concrete implementation lesson;
- a measurable business idea;
- a credible contrarian view grounded in experience.

Avoid generic:
- “AI will change everything” posts;
- rewritten AI news;
- top-10 tool lists without original insight;
- engagement bait.
