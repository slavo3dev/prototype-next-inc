# Ideal Customer Profile

## Initial market

Prototype.NEXT should initially focus on the United States.

## Company size

Primary range:
**20–200 employees**

This range is large enough to contain:
- operational inefficiency;
- expensive manual workflows;
- meaningful software budgets;
- growing data complexity.

It is also often small enough for executive buyers to move faster than large-enterprise procurement.

## Priority company types

- SaaS;
- media;
- agencies;
- e-commerce;
- technology companies;
- professional services;
- data-heavy businesses;
- businesses with meaningful internal operations and repeated knowledge work.

## Strong buying signals

A company is attractive when one or more of these are true:

- employees repeatedly copy information between systems;
- teams spend hours researching internal information;
- support volume is increasing;
- operational headcount grows linearly with revenue;
- management wants AI but lacks a production roadmap;
- the company has experimented with LLMs but cannot operationalize them;
- engineering is overloaded with repetitive work;
- internal knowledge is fragmented;
- customer-facing AI features are strategically important;
- leadership wants measurable productivity improvement;
- the company has proprietary data that can create an AI advantage.

## Buyers

### CEO / Founder

Cares about:
- growth;
- leverage;
- strategic differentiation;
- speed;
- margin;
- competitive advantage.

### COO

Cares about:
- workflows;
- efficiency;
- throughput;
- operational cost;
- automation;
- reliability.

### CTO / VP Engineering

Cares about:
- architecture;
- security;
- maintainability;
- integration;
- technical risk;
- delivery capacity.

### Head of Product

Cares about:
- AI-enabled product capabilities;
- customer value;
- speed to validation;
- product differentiation.

## Poor-fit customers

Avoid prioritizing:
- businesses looking only for the cheapest chatbot;
- prospects with no meaningful process or budget;
- buyers asking for AI solely because it is fashionable;
- clients expecting fully autonomous systems where risk requires human oversight;
- projects without a measurable outcome;
- tiny one-off website jobs unrelated to the strategic direction.

## Initial outreach thesis

Target executive conversations around:

**What expensive workflow inside your company still depends on humans searching, copying, classifying, summarizing, routing, or repeatedly making the same type of decision?**

That question leads directly into the AI Transformation Sprint.
