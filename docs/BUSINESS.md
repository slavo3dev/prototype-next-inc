# Business

## Company

Prototype.NEXT is an AI Transformation & Production Engineering company.

The company exists to help businesses move from interest in AI to measurable operational and product outcomes.

## Core business thesis

Most companies do not need more AI demos.

They need help answering four questions:

1. Where can AI create meaningful economic value?
2. Which use cases are feasible and worth prioritizing?
3. How should the system be engineered safely and reliably?
4. How does the company move from prototype to production and continuous improvement?

Prototype.NEXT addresses all four.

## Business model

### Stage 1 — AI Transformation

We enter through a focused transformation engagement.

We study:
- workflows;
- repeated decisions;
- information bottlenecks;
- manual data movement;
- knowledge retrieval;
- customer support;
- engineering processes;
- operational handoffs;
- high-latency internal processes.

The goal is to identify high-value opportunities rather than force AI into every process.

### Stage 2 — Implementation

Once an opportunity is validated, Prototype.NEXT engineers the production system.

Implementation may include:
- agents;
- RAG;
- internal knowledge systems;
- AI APIs;
- workflow orchestration;
- data pipelines;
- integrations;
- user interfaces;
- backend systems;
- evaluation;
- monitoring;
- governance;
- human approval flows.

### Stage 3 — Continuous partnership

Deployed AI systems need:
- monitoring;
- evaluation;
- model upgrades;
- cost optimization;
- prompt and architecture tuning;
- workflow expansion;
- new integrations;
- reliability improvements.

This creates recurring revenue and a long-term client relationship.

### Stage 4 — Productization

Repeated client problems become product candidates.

Prototype.NEXT should not guess which SaaS product to build.

The service business is used as a market-intelligence engine. When the same valuable problem repeatedly appears across customers, Prototype.NEXT can convert a proven internal capability into software.

## Revenue architecture

Initial revenue mix:

1. AI Transformation Sprint — $15K–$30K
2. Production implementation — approximately $25K–$100K+ depending on scope
3. Ongoing AI Systems Partnership — approximately $5K–$20K+ monthly depending on scope
4. Product revenue — later, only after repeated validated demand

These are positioning ranges, not fixed public pricing.

## Why this model

The model creates four advantages:

- near-term consulting revenue;
- real-world AI engineering experience;
- direct access to customer problems;
- a path toward scalable software products.

## Strategic constraint

Do not build unrelated standalone products simply because they are technically interesting.

New products should be justified by:
- repeated customer pain;
- measurable willingness to pay;
- reusable architecture;
- a large enough market;
- clear differentiation.

## Long-term destination

Prototype.NEXT should evolve from a services-led company into a hybrid:

- AI transformation;
- production engineering;
- recurring AI systems partnerships;
- proprietary AI products built from repeated market patterns.
