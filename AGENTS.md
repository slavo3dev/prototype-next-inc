1. Read this file.

## Company

**Prototype.NEXT** is an **AI Transformation & Production Engineering** company.

We help established companies identify high-value AI opportunities, redesign workflows, build the required software and AI systems, and put those systems into production.

### Core idea

**From AI opportunity to production.**

Prototype.NEXT is not positioned as:
- a chatbot agency;
- a generic automation shop;
- a prompt-engineering consultancy;
- a generic outsourced software-development agency;
- an OpenAI-only consultancy.

We are engineering-first, business-first, model-agnostic, and focused on measurable outcomes.

## Business model

Prototype.NEXT follows this progression:

1. **AI Transformation**
   - Discover business problems and workflow opportunities.
   - Prioritize opportunities by value, feasibility, risk, and time-to-impact.

2. **AI Engineering**
   - Architect and implement production AI systems.
   - Integrate AI into existing products and operations.

3. **Production Engineering**
   - Build the software, data, APIs, interfaces, infrastructure, observability, and reliability layer required to ship.

4. **Continuous AI Systems Partnership**
   - Improve, evaluate, monitor, optimize, and expand deployed systems.

5. **Productization**
   - Identify repeated customer problems and convert proven patterns into scalable products.

## Primary offer

The initial front-door offer is the **AI Transformation Sprint**.

Target commercial range:
- **$15K–$30K** for the sprint.
- Larger implementation work follows when a validated opportunity is identified.

The sprint should produce:
- workflow discovery;
- opportunity map;
- prioritized use cases;
- architecture;
- working prototype or implementation where appropriate;
- ROI model;
- implementation roadmap;
- risk and governance considerations.

## Engineering principles

- Production before novelty.
- Business value before model hype.
- Simple architectures before unnecessary orchestration.
- Human-in-the-loop when risk warrants it.
- Model-agnostic design where practical.
- Evaluation must be designed with the system, not added at the end.
- Security, privacy, reliability, and observability are first-class concerns.
- Prefer maintainable, explicit systems over opaque complexity.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
- Measure latency, cost, quality, reliability, and business impact.
